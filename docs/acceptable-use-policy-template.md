## [Organisation name] email acceptable-use policy

- **Version:** [version]
- **Effective date:** [date]
- **Policy owner:** [role or team]
- **Report concerns:** [private contact or reporting process]

### 1. Purpose

This policy describes acceptable use of email accounts, personal mailboxes, shared mailboxes, and related records operated by [Organisation name] through [mail domain].

### 2. Scope

This policy applies to every user, administrator, contractor, service account, and authorised system that accesses or sends through the service.

### 3. Acceptable use

Users may use the service for lawful, authorised communication connected with their role. Users must:

- communicate honestly, respectfully, and professionally;
- identify themselves and their organisation accurately;
- protect confidential and personal information;
- use approved mailbox assignments and sender addresses;
- follow applicable consent, marketing, privacy, retention, and records rules;
- report suspected compromise, misdelivery, or abuse promptly.

### 4. Prohibited use

Users must not:

- send spam or use scraped, purchased, or unauthorised recipient lists;
- phish, impersonate, defraud, threaten, harass, or distribute unlawful content;
- distribute malware or intentionally unsafe attachments or links;
- bypass authentication, authorization, audit, retention, or delivery controls;
- disclose personal, confidential, regulated, or privileged information without authority;
- use an unapproved service to send as an organisation domain;
- access another user's or mailbox's information without a valid assignment and purpose;
- interfere with service availability or another user's work.

### 5. Shared mailboxes

Shared mailbox access is granted according to operational need. Users may exercise only the read, send-as, or full permission assigned to them. Access must be reviewed when responsibilities change and removed promptly when no longer required.

### 6. Security responsibilities

Users must protect their identity-provider account, use multi-factor authentication where required, keep devices reasonably secure, and never share sessions or credentials. Suspected account compromise must be reported through [security process].

### 7. Privacy, monitoring, and records

The service records message metadata and administrative activity required to operate, secure, and govern the system. [Organisation name] will define what is collected, who can access it, how long it is retained, and how lawful requests are handled in its privacy and records policies.

Users should have no expectation that organisational mail is private from authorised operational, legal, security, or compliance review, subject to applicable law and policy.

### 8. Deliverability and fair use

Users must send only volumes appropriate to authorised activity and must respect opt-out, complaint, and correction requests. Activity that threatens domain reputation, provider standing, service capacity, or recipient safety may be limited or suspended.

### 9. Enforcement

Suspected breaches will be reviewed fairly and proportionately. Responses may include guidance, access restriction, mailbox suspension, disciplinary or contractual action, preservation of evidence, or referral to relevant authorities. Emergency containment may occur before a full review when necessary to protect people, data, or service availability.

### 10. Reporting

Report suspected abuse, privacy incidents, misdirected mail, compromised accounts, or unsafe requests through [private reporting process]. Good-faith reports should be protected from retaliation.

### 11. Changes and acknowledgement

Material changes will be versioned and communicated. Users may be required to acknowledge a new version before continuing to access mail.

### Acknowledgement

I confirm that I have read and understood this policy and agree to follow it while using [Organisation name] email services.
